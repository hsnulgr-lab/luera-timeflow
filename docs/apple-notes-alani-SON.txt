The demo account, its password and all flows from 1.0 are unchanged.

SCREEN RECORDING: attached below (Attachment field), made on a physical
device with the earlier build 1.0.0 (4); sign-in, daily flow, registration
and deletion work the same in 1.0.1. Recorded at 00:50, before opening, so

DEMO ACCOUNT: see Sign-In Information above. Pre-loaded salon with staff,
services, packages, customers and a full day of appointments. Subscription
permanently active - no paywall or locked screen.

WHAT IT IS. Appointment and day-management app for hair and beauty salons,
barbers, nail and tattoo studios. Publicly available - any salon owner in
Turkey can sign up in the app. Not an internal or employee-only tool.

NO PURCHASES - 3.1.3(f). No in-app purchase, no price, no plan, no upgrade
prompt, no call to action anywhere. Subscriptions are handled on the web by
the owner; the app never links to or mentions them. Locked by a test in our
repository.

HOW TO REVIEW. Bottom tabs: Akis (day flow), Takvim (calendar), Randevu
(new appointment), Kasa (till), Profil. Customers: Profil > Musteriler.
Packages: Profil > Paketler.
Staff: Profil > Personel. Legal: Profil > Yasal. Public booking page for
end customers, no login: https://timeflow.lueratech.com/book/demo-luera

ACCOUNT DELETION. Profil > Hesap > "Hesabimi sil" - real server-side
deletion. PLEASE TEST IT WITH A NEW ACCOUNT: the demo account owns the demo
salon. Tap "Yeni isletme olustur" on the welcome screen and sign up with any
email - you are signed in at once. The last step asks who does the work;
either answer is fine. Then delete that account (in one-person mode:
Isletme > Hesap > "Hesabimi sil").

ONE-PERSON MODE (new). Answering "Yalniz ben" (Just me) at the end of
sign-up opens a simpler app for a salon of one: Gun (day), Takvim, Randevu,
Kasa, Isletme. The owner starts and finishes each job and records how the
customer paid at the counter (Nakit / Kart / Havale = cash / card / bank
transfer). This only records money received in person; no payment is
processed in the app.

STAFF MODE. Welcome screen > "Burada calisiyorum". Employees see only their
own day, never the salon's totals, and sign in with a 15-minute team code
from the owner, so no static credential can be given. Optional, as it
replaces the demo session: Profil > Personel > "Telefon bagla" (code) >
Profil > Hesap > "Oturumu kapat" > "Burada calisiyorum" > enter the code >
pick a name > set a password.

EXTERNAL SERVICES. Self-hosted Supabase (our own VPS) for database, auth
and server functions; Expo Push / Apple APNs for notifications; self-hosted
Evolution API for WhatsApp reminders from the salon's own line (server-side
only); Dodo Payments for the web subscription, not reachable from the app.
No analytics, no advertising, no tracking. Reminder wording may be drafted
on our server by Google Gemini or Groq from non-personal inputs only
(service type, time, salon name). Customer names, phone numbers and
messages are never sent to any AI service. The optional WhatsApp assistant
is off.

USER CONTENT. The only free-text fields are private notes a salon writes
about its own appointments and customers, visible only inside that salon's
account and never shared, so no reporting or blocking is needed.

REGULATED INDUSTRY. None. Turkey only, Turkish interface. No medical
service or health claim; dental and clinical sectors are excluded from
sign-up. App Privacy lists "Health & Fitness" only because a customer note
may mention a product allergy; there is no health field.

THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL. None; all content is our own. Typeface: Hanken
Grotesk, SIL Open Font License.

WHATSAPP. No line is connected in the demo salon, so the WhatsApp button
is dimmed and explains why.

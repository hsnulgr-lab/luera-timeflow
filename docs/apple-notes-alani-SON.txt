DEMO ACCOUNT: see Sign-In Information above. Pre-loaded salon with staff,
services, packages, customers and a full day of appointments. Subscription
permanently active - no paywall or locked screen.

WHAT IT IS. Appointment and day-management app for hair and beauty salons,
barbers, nail and tattoo studios. The owner sees the day live; employees
see only their own work. Publicly available - any salon owner in Turkey can
sign up from inside the app. Not an internal or employee-only tool. No
medical or health service.

NO PURCHASES - 3.1.3(f). No in-app purchase, no price, no plan, no upgrade
prompt, no call to action anywhere. Subscriptions are handled on the web by
the owner; the app never links to or mentions them. Locked by a test in our
repository.

HOW TO REVIEW. Bottom tabs: Akis (day flow), Takvim (calendar), Randevu
(new appointment), Kasa (till), Profil. Customer book: Profil > Musteriler.
Staff: Profil > Personel. Legal: Profil > Yasal. Public booking page used by
end customers, no login: https://timeflow.lueratech.com/book/demo-luera

ACCOUNT DELETION. Profil > Hesap > "Hesabimi sil". Real server-side
deletion, not a local sign-out. PLEASE TEST IT WITH A NEW ACCOUNT: the demo
account is the sole owner of the demo salon, so deleting it removes the
whole salon. Tap "Yeni isletme olustur" on the welcome screen, sign up with
any email - you are signed in at once - and delete that account.

STAFF MODE (second account type). Welcome screen > "Burada calisiyorum".
Employees see only their own day, never the salon's totals. They sign in
with a 15-minute team code from the owner, so no static credential can be
given here. To try it: Profil > Personel > "Telefon bagla" (a code appears)
> Profil > Hesap > "Oturumu kapat" > "Burada calisiyorum" > enter the code
> pick a name under "Siz kimsiniz?" > set a password. This replaces the
demo manager session, so it is optional - screenshots 3 and 7 show this
mode. If anything does not work, please contact us.

EXTERNAL SERVICES. Self-hosted Supabase (our own VPS) for database, auth
and server functions; Expo Push / Apple APNs for notifications; self-hosted
Evolution API for WhatsApp reminders from the salon's own line (server-side
only); Dodo Payments for the web subscription, not reachable from the app.
No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no AI service.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT. The only free-text fields are private notes a salon
writes about its own appointments and customers. Visible only inside that
salon's account, never published, never shared. No user can see another
user's content, so no reporting or blocking mechanism is required.

REGIONS. None. Single Turkish interface, Turkey only, identical for all.

REGULATED INDUSTRY. None. No medical service, no health claim, not a
medical device. Dental and clinical sectors are excluded from sign-up. Our
App Privacy declaration includes "Health & Fitness" only because a salon's
free-text customer note may contain a product allergy; there is no health
screen or field in the app.

THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL. None. All text, icons and illustrations are our own.
Typeface: Hanken Grotesk, SIL Open Font License.

WHATSAPP. The demo salon has no line connected, so the WhatsApp button on
appointment cards appears dimmed and explains where it is connected.
Expected, not a defect.

Turkish labels: Akis=Flow, Takvim=Calendar, Randevu=Appointment, Kasa=Till,
Musteriler=Customers, Personel=Staff, Profil=Profile, Hesap=Account,
Yasal=Legal, Geldi=Customer arrived, Oturumu kapat=Sign out.

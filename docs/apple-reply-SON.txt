2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
An appointment and day-management app for small appointment-based
businesses: hair and beauty salons, barbers, nail and tattoo studios. They
still run the day on a paper book at the reception desk, so anyone not
standing there cannot see what is happening - which causes double bookings,
missed arrivals and unrecorded payments. The app gives the owner that same
day live in their pocket, and gives employees a simpler mode showing only
their own work. It is publicly available: any salon owner in Turkey can
download it and create a business account from inside the app. It is not
built for one company and is not an internal or employee-only tool.

3. SETUP AND ACCESS
Credentials for the demo manager account are in the Sign-In Information
field of this submission. That account is pre-loaded with a demo salon and
a permanently active subscription, so no paywall appears. Bottom tabs: Akis
(day flow), Takvim (calendar), Randevu (new appointment), Kasa (till),
Profil. Customer book: Profil > Musteriler. Staff: Profil > Personel.
Delete account: Profil > Hesap > "Hesabimi sil". The second account type
(staff mode) is reached from the welcome screen with "Burada calisiyorum";
step-by-step instructions are in the Notes field. Public booking page used
by end customers, no login: https://timeflow.lueratech.com/book/demo-luera

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
Self-hosted Supabase on our own VPS (database, authentication, server
functions); Expo Push / Apple APNs for notifications only; self-hosted
Evolution API for WhatsApp reminders sent from the salon's own line,
server-side only; Dodo Payments for the web subscription, which is not
reachable from the app. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no AI
service. The only free-text fields are private notes a salon writes about
its own appointments and customers, visible only inside that salon's own
account and never published or shared between businesses.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. Single Turkish interface, offered only in Turkey, identical behaviour
for every user.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY AND THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not a regulated industry: no medical or health service, no health claim,
not a medical device. Dental and clinical sectors are excluded from
sign-up. Our App Privacy declaration includes "Health & Fitness" only
because a salon's free-text customer note may contain a product allergy;
there is no health screen or field anywhere in the app. No third-party protected material: all text, icons and
illustrations are our own, and the typeface is Hanken Grotesk under the SIL
Open Font License.
